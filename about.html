About Enchanted Locks

Unleash your most vibrant self in a safe, joyful space.

Our Story

Enchanted Locks was founded by master hairdresser Nina Thompson. Built on a foundation of unyielding positivity, infectious laughter, and exceptional styling craftsmanship, our salon is a safe haven where individuals come to unleash their most vibrant selves.

After a decade of working in traditional salons, Nina decided to build a creative studio where bold self-expression isn't just accepted—it is celebrated! Our physical salon is a warm, joyful environment filled with good music, endless laughter, and highly customized styling chairs.

What We Offer Behind the Chair

  • Premium Styling: Every precision cut and dramatic colour shift is executed using industry-leading, vegan, and cruelty-free products.
  • Empowering Advice: We don't just fix your hair; we sit with you to provide personalized beauty tips, healthy routine guides, and seamless booking options.

Who We Proudly Welcome

  • Trend Seekers: Perfect for people who are passionate about hair fashion, bold self-expression, and modern aesthetic beauty.
  • All Hair Profiles: We are proudly inclusive! Come to us with straight, wavy, curly, coily, fine, or ultra-thick hair types.
  • All Generations: We treat teenagers, adults, and clients of all ages who want a supportive environment to find their next signature look.
