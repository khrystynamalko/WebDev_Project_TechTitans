Nina's Top Beauty & Care Tips

1. Protect Color from Heat

Always apply a lightweight thermal heat protectant spray before blow-drying or using hot styling tools to keep your vibrant shades fresh.

2. Scalp Health Matters

Healthy hair starts at the root! Use a soft silicone scalp scrubber once a week to gently exfoliate product buildup and promote circulation.

3. Hydrate & Repair

Switch to a sulfate-free, vegan shampoo and indulge in a weekly leave-in moisture mask to prevent split ends between salon visits.
